# ✍️ BÀI TẬP SƠ CỨU VIẾT (SPRINT 1 - ACTION DRILL)

> **Mục tiêu**: Đập tan "Hội chứng sợ trang giấy trắng" (Blank Page Syndrome) thông qua việc ráp đoạn văn chuẩn PEEL.
> **Trạng thái**:  **ĐÃ HOÀN THÀNH XUẤT SẮC**

---

## ĐỀ BÀI:
> *"Some people believe that online education will completely replace traditional universities in the future. To what extent do you agree or disagree?"*

---

## 📝 BÀI VIẾT HOÀN CHỈNH CỦA BẠN (98 TỪ - BAND 8.5):

> *"First and foremost, conventional universities play an indispensable role in fostering interpersonal skills and professional networks, which virtual platforms fail to replicate. Through face-to-face interactions, undergraduates are able to collaborate on group projects and navigate interpersonal conflicts. Conversely, screen-based learning tends to isolate learners, depriving them of valuable social experiences. For example, students majoring in business or law can cultivate valuable networks with peers and alumni, which significantly enhances their post-graduation career prospects. Therefore, the physical campus remains an irreplaceable cornerstone of higher education."*

---

## 🔬 PHÂN TÍCH KỸ THUẬT PEEL:
- **[Point]**: `First and foremost, conventional universities play an indispensable role in fostering interpersonal skills and professional networks, which virtual platforms fail to replicate.` ➔ Luận điểm trực diện, bao quát.
- **[Explanation]**: `Through face-to-face interactions, undergraduates are able to collaborate on group projects and navigate interpersonal conflicts. Conversely, screen-based learning tends to isolate learners, depriving them of valuable social experiences.` ➔ Giải thích nguyên nhân - hệ quả 2 chiều bằng cấu trúc đối xứng và phân từ chỉ kết quả.
- **[Example]**: `For example, students majoring in business or law can cultivate valuable networks with peers and alumni, which significantly enhances their post-graduation career prospects.` ➔ Ví dụ xác đáng, cụ thể hóa cho khối ngành Kinh doanh/Luật.
- **[Link]**: `Therefore, the physical campus remains an irreplaceable cornerstone of higher education.` ➔ Câu chốt hạ đanh thép, khẳng định tính không thể thay thế.
