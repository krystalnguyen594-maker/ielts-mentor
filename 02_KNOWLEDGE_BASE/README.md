# 🧠 KNOWLEDGE BASE (KHO TRI THỨC IELTS CHUYÊN SÂU)

Kho lưu trữ tài nguyên nạp Input học thuật chất lượng cao để bứt phá lên Band 7.5 - 8.0:

```
02_KNOWLEDGE_BASE/
├── Grammar_Precision/    # Các cấu trúc ngữ pháp đòn bẩy (Inversion, Cleft sentences, Participles...)
├── Lexical_Resource/     # 10 bộ Collocation ngân hàng theo chủ đề học thuật lớn
└── Idea_Bank/            # Bản đồ tư duy luận điểm 2 chiều (Arguments for & against)
```

## 10 CHỦ ĐỀ HỌC THUẬT CỐT LÕI (CORE THEMES)
1. **Education**: Formal vs informal schooling, technology in classrooms, curriculum design.
2. **Technology & AI**: Automation, ethics of artificial intelligence, privacy and surveillance.
3. **Environment & Energy**: Climate change, renewable transition, individual vs governmental responsibility.
4. **Society & Urbanisation**: Megacities, public transportation, affordable housing, aging populations.
5. **Work & Employment**: Remote work, work-life balance, job satisfaction vs salary.
6. **Health & Healthcare**: Preventative healthcare vs curative medicine, mental health, obesity crises.
7. **Culture & Globalisation**: Cultural homogenisation, preservation of indigenous languages and heritage.
8. **Government & Economy**: Taxation, funding for arts vs scientific research, international aid.
9. **Media & Advertising**: Digital consumerism, news credibility, influencer culture.
10. **Crime & Justice**: Rehabilitation vs punitive measures, youth delinquency, capital punishment.
