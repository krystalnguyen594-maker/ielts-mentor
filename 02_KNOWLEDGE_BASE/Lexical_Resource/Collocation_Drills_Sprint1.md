## 💎 PHẦN 2: 10 CẶP COLLOCATION "VÀNG" NÂNG BAND 7.5+
*(Học viên thường dùng từ đơn giản ở bên trái, giám khảo 7.5+ kỳ vọng các cụm ở bên phải)*

| Bạn hay nói (Band 5.5 - 6.0) | Cụm Collocation Học thuật (Band 7.5 - 8.0) | Ví dụ ngữ cảnh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *solve a problem* | **tackle / address a pressing issue** | *Governments must address this pressing issue immediately.* |
| *have a bad effect on* | **pose a grave threat to / exert a detrimental impact on** | *Industrial waste poses a grave threat to marine ecosystems.* |
| *bring good results* | **yield tangible benefits** | *Investing in early education yields tangible economic benefits.* |
| *make things equal* | **bridge the wealth gap / socioeconomic disparity** | *Progressive tax policies aim to bridge the wealth gap.* |
| *help develop* | **foster / nurture (innovation, critical thinking)** | *Interactive classrooms help foster critical thinking.* |
| *take duty/responsibility* | **shoulder the responsibility** | *Both parents and educators must shoulder the responsibility.* |
| *cause many debates* | **spark fierce controversy / ignite public debate** | *The proposed AI regulations have ignited intense public debate.* |
| *open opportunities* | **pave the way for (groundbreaking advances)** | *Renewable tech will pave the way for sustainable cities.* |
| *get the reward/gain* | **reap the rewards / harvest the fruits of...** | *Diligent students will eventually reap the rewards of their labor.* |
| *very important reason* | **a compelling rationale** | *There is a compelling rationale for transitioning to solar energy.* |
